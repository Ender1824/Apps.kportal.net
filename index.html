Universe Daily

On the App Store · Free · Education

The universe, daily.

A daily window into space: NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day, live data on who and what is in orbit, and the launches, eclipses and news worth looking up for. Plus space games like Constellation Quiz and Cosmic Cipher.

  • Picture of the DayA new image from NASA every morning.
  • ISS TrackerThe ISS and Tiangong on a live 3D globe.
  • People in SpaceWho's in orbit now, and every astronaut ever.
  • Upcoming LaunchesLaunches worldwide, with live countdowns.
  • Night SkyDark skies, aurora forecast and the Moon.
  • EclipsesEvery solar and lunar eclipse through 2050.

Frutiger Sports

Coming soon to the App Store · Sports games

Swing your phone. Play the sport.

Table tennis, golf, bowling and archery in a sunny, glossy world, where your iPhone is the paddle, the club, the ball and the bow. Every game reads the angle, speed and twist of your wrist.

  • Table TennisBrush for topspin, chop to slice, twist to curve.
  • GolfTurn your wrist to hook or slice, and read the wind.
  • BowlingGrip, swing and release. Twist to hook it.
  • ArcheryDraw back, hold steady, allow for drop and wind.
  • Play NearbyTwo iPhones on the same Wi‑Fi, head to head.
  • Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no tracking.
